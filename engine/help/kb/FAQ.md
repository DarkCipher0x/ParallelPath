# ParallelPath FAQ

Short answers to common questions. The in-app Help & FAQ (the ? button) searches these and the user guide, on your
device.

## Privacy and security

### Can the server or its owner read our messages?
No. Everything you share is encrypted on your device before it leaves it, and only the devices of the people it's
shared with can decrypt it. The server stores ciphertext. Its owner can see who is in which family and when things
were sent, but never what was written.

### What does post-quantum mean?
The encryption uses the newest NIST standards (ML-KEM-1024 and ML-DSA-87) together with classical cryptography. It
stays secure even against future quantum computers, as long as either half holds.

### Can anything be deleted or changed without anyone knowing?
No. You can edit or withdraw something, but you must give a reason, and the earlier version stays in its history.
Every action is signed and chained into the audit log; if anyone, the server included, tried to rewrite it, every
family member's app would detect it.

### Does the tone check or the help send my text anywhere?
No. Both run inside ParallelPath on your device. There's no cloud AI service, and they work offline.

### Is the other parent able to read my journal?
No. My journal is private to you. Nobody else can read it, or even tell it exists, unless you share an entry.

## Messages and conversations

### How do I message just one person privately?
In Messages, choose New private conversation, tick the person (or people) and Start. Only the people in it (and you)
can read it: not the rest of the family, and not the server. The family's permissions decide who you may message.

### Who can read a private conversation?
Only its people. It has its own encryption keys, given only to their devices. If someone leaves the family, the
conversation's keys change so they can't read anything new.

### Why can't I start a conversation with someone?
The parent who invited you chose who you may message (for example, only the parents), or the other person isn't
allowed to message you. Ask the parent who invited you; they can change it in Family › your name › Permissions.

### How do I edit or withdraw a message?
Open the message's menu and choose Edit or Withdraw. Both need a reason, which everyone can see in the message's
history together with the original.

### What is the tone check?
Before something goes into the family's record, ParallelPath looks for wording that may come across as hostile, such
as insults, blame, threats, shouting in capitals or "!!!", and suggests a calmer way to put it. You can take the
calmer version, change it yourself, or send it as it is. Turn it off in Settings.

### Why did the tone check warn me about a normal message?
It's careful on purpose and sometimes flags blunt but fair wording. It never stops you sending; choose Send as it
is.

## Writing to the other parent

### How should I write to the other parent?
Keep it brief, informative, friendly and firm. Stick to one topic and the facts (dates, times, costs), say what you
need and by when, and leave out the past and opinions about them. Read it as if a judge will, because one might.

### How do I reply to an angry or hostile message?
You don't have to answer straight away. Reply only to the practical part (the arrangement, the date, the cost), in a
few calm sentences, and ignore insults and accusations. If there's nothing practical to answer, a short
acknowledgement is enough, or no reply. Everything stays in the record either way.

### How do I say no to a request?
Say no clearly and briefly, give a short reason, and offer an alternative if there is one: "I can't do Saturday
because of Mia's party, but Sunday from 10 works." Avoid long justifications; they invite argument.

### How do I raise a problem without starting an argument?
Describe what happened and what you'd like next time, not what kind of person they are: "Pick-up was 40 minutes
late on Friday. Can we agree to text if you're running more than 15 minutes late?"

### Should I mention the children's feelings in messages?
Share what the children need to be looked after (health, school, worries they've raised), plainly and without using
them as an argument. Never ask the children to pass messages between you.

### What does the tone bar under the message box mean?
It shows how your draft may come across as you type: green is calm, amber is careful, red is likely to read as
hostile. It's worked out on your device. Choose Advice to see what to change and a calmer version.

### Can I get advice on what to write?
Yes. Choose Advice by the message box: it shows what may come across badly, a calmer version, tips for writing to
a co-parent, and you can ask a question. Everything is worked out on your device.

## Calls

### How do I call someone?
Use the phone or camera button at the top of the family and choose who to call. Only their devices ring. In a
private conversation, use Voice call or Video call there.

### Who can my child call?
By default, children can call and message only their parents. The parent who invited the child can change that in
Family › the child › Permissions: anyone, the parents, chosen people or nobody, and video or voice only.

### Can calls be recorded?
ParallelPath never records calls. A call shows in the conversation afterwards (who called, when and for how long),
but its sound and pictures are never stored.

### Are calls encrypted?
Yes. Every audio and video frame is encrypted end to end with a key only the people in the call have. The server's
relay forwards what it can't read.

### Why can't I ring the whole family?
Ringing everyone at once is for parents, and for people a parent allowed it. Choose individual people instead.

## Family, invites and permissions

### How do I invite someone?
Family › Invite someone: choose their role, check What they can do, and Create invite code. Send the code
privately; it works once, for 7 days. Then approve them after comparing safety numbers.

### What permissions can I give someone I invite?
Which sections they see (family chat, calendar, expenses, documents, info bank), which they can add to, who they may
message privately and who they may call, whether video calls are allowed, and whether they can ring the whole family.
Only you, as the inviting parent, can change them later. Every change is recorded in the audit log.

### Can the other parent change permissions for someone I invited?
No. Only the parent who invited someone can change their permissions. The other parent can see them.

### What is a safety number and why compare it?
It's a 60-digit number that both your apps work out from your keys. If it matches when you compare it on a call or in
person, nobody is impersonating either of you. Approve someone only after it matches.

### How do I invite a mediator or solicitor?
Invite them as a Professional. Choose how long their access lasts and how much history they see (all, last year,
last 3 months, or from today). They're read-only, and their access ends automatically.

### Can the other parent remove me?
No. Neither approved parent can remove the other. If that's ever genuinely needed, the server's owner can block an
account.

### Can children write in the family chat?
Only if a parent allows it: either Children can send messages on the Family tab, or the inviting parent gives them
"add to family chat" in their permissions.

### Can I start another family or invite another family onto the server?
No - only the server's owner (its administrator) can bring a new family onto the server. Your server invite let you
start one family; you can invite people into it, but not start more families or make server invites. If you need a
second family space, ask the server's owner: they can allow it in the admin portal.

## Documents and files

### How do I organise files into folders?
Documents › New folder, then drag files onto it, or right-click a file › Move to…. Cut and paste (Ctrl+X, Ctrl+V) work
too. Moving a folder moves everything in it. A move needs a short reason, which stays in the file's history.

### What can I do when I right-click a file?
Open it, open it in another app, save a copy, send it in a message, cut, copy, move, copy to another folder or
family, rename, upload a new version, edit its note, copy its name or path, see its history and properties, or remove
it. On a phone, press and hold instead of right-clicking.

### How do I select several files at once?
Ctrl+click to add files, Shift+click to select a range, Ctrl+A for everything. On a phone, press and hold a file and
choose Select, then tap the others. Then right-click for what you can do with all of them.

### Can I upload by dragging files in?
Yes, on Windows: drag files from File Explorer onto the Documents screen. They're encrypted on your computer and
uploaded to the folder you're in.

### How do I save a file onto my computer or phone?
Right-click it › Save a copy… (or Ctrl+S). The copy is a normal, unencrypted file where you choose to put it. Saving a
copy counts as opening the file, so it's recorded.

### Where are the photos and files people sent in messages?
Documents › Shared in messages lists every file from the family's messages and your private conversations. Right-click
one › Save to Documents to file it in a folder. In a conversation you can also right-click a message › Save to
Documents.

### Why can't I rename or move someone else's file?
Only the person who added a file can change it - that keeps the record trustworthy. You can copy it (right-click ›
Copy to…) and do what you like with your copy.

### Can I get back a file I removed?
Yes. Documents › Removed lists everything removed; right-click your file › Restore, with a reason. Removed files can
always still be opened.

### Can I copy files into my other family?
Yes, if you're in more than one family: right-click › Copy to…, choose Copy, then the other family and a folder. The
file is encrypted again with that family's keys.

## Calendar and money

### How do I set up a parenting schedule?
Calendar › Schedule › Propose a new schedule: choose a common pattern or Custom, who starts, the start date and the
handover time. It applies once the other parent agrees.

### How do I ask to swap days?
Tap a day, then Ask for a change. To ask for several dates in one go, choose Ask for another period in the same
request. The other parent agrees or declines with a reason.

### How do expenses work?
Add what you paid, with receipts and the share you're asking for. The other parent agrees or declines, records their
payment, and you confirm it arrived. The balance shows who owes whom.

### How do reminders work?
Give an event a reminder. ParallelPath notifies you at that time while the app is unlocked (on Android, while it's
running).

## Reports

### How do I make a report for court?
Audit log › Court-ready report: choose the dates and sections. You get a PDF to read, a signed file that is the
evidence, and a spreadsheet copy. You can include your private conversations.

### How can a court check a report?
In the app, Audit log › Verify a report, or with the server's command ppserver verify-report on the signed file. One
changed character anywhere makes it fail.

## Devices and account

### How do I add my phone or another computer?
On a device you already use: Settings › Link another device. On the new device choose I already use ParallelPath,
enter the code, and confirm on the first device. The new device gets everything, including your journal.

### I forgot my passphrase. What can I do?
Nobody can reset it. Link the device again from another device of yours, or use your recovery code.

### What is a recovery code?
Settings › Make a recovery code shows a code to write down and keep offline. If you lose every device, choose
Recover my account on a new one. Your old devices are signed out, and the family's parents approve the new device.

### My phone was stolen. What should I do?
Ask the server's owner to revoke it (it can then never sign in again). The family's keys change automatically so it
can't read anything new. Then link a new device or use your recovery code.

### Why do I get a certificate warning in my browser?
Your server uses its own certificate authority. Open https://your-server/trust once on that device and follow the
steps. The Windows and Android apps don't need this.

### Which devices can I use?
Windows 10 and 11, Android 10 and newer, and any current browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) through the web app
on your server.

## Notifications

### Why don't notifications show the message?
They can appear on a locked screen, so by default they only say who wrote and what kind of thing it is. Turn on Show
message text in notifications in Settings if you prefer.

### I'm not getting notifications.
Check they're on in Settings, and that Windows, Android or your browser allows notifications for ParallelPath.
They come while the app is unlocked.
