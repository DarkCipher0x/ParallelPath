# ParallelPath changelog

## 0.0.1 (first release, beta)

The first release of ParallelPath: free, self-hosted co-parenting with a record you can trust, end-to-end encrypted
with post-quantum cryptography. Apps for Windows, Android and the web; a server for Windows or Ubuntu.

**Communication**
- Messages with photos, videos, files, reactions and replies; read receipts from the signed audit log; edits and
  withdrawals only with a reason, every version kept.
- Private conversations with one or more people in the family, with keys of their own.
- An on-device tone check, a live tone bar and Advice on any draft (a calmer version, tips, questions answered from
  the built-in help). No cloud AI.
- HD voice and video calls with end-to-end encrypted frames and screen sharing; ring the whole family or chosen
  people. Never recorded.
- Notifications and event reminders.

**Parenting time and money**
- A shared calendar coloured by who has the children; common and custom schedules applied only when both parents
  agree; change requests; events with reminders; check-ins at handovers.
- Expenses with receipts and a requested share; agree or decline, record and confirm payments, a running balance.

**Records**
- Documents as a full file explorer: folder tree, Recent, Shared in messages and Removed; details and icon views,
  search, multi-select, right-click menus, cut/copy/paste, drag and drop, versions, restore, saved copies and an
  access log.
- Right-click (press-and-hold) menus on messages, attachments, expenses, info bank and journal entries, calendar
  events and family members.
- The info bank for each child, and a private journal only its author can read.
- Signed court-ready reports (PDF, JSON, spreadsheet) tied to a hash-chained audit log anyone can verify.

**People and access**
- Parents, family members, children and professionals (read-only, time-limited, optionally limited to recent
  history); permissions per person, set by the parent who invites them; safety-number approval for every person and
  device; device linking and recovery codes.
- Only the server's owner can bring new families onto a server.

**Server**
- A setup wizard for Windows and a terminal installer for Ubuntu; encrypted storage required; its own certificate
  authority or Let's Encrypt; a call relay; daily backups; a web admin portal.
- The security test report (PENTEST-0.0.1) lists every finding from testing and how it was fixed.
