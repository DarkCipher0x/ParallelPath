# Privacy

ParallelPath is built so that nobody but your family can read what you share - not the server, not its owner, and
not DarkCipher Systems.

## What stays on your devices

- Everything you write and share (messages, calendar, expenses, documents, the info bank, your journal, call
  audio and video) is encrypted on your device before it leaves, with keys only your family's approved devices hold.
- Names, including your own and the family's name, are encrypted in the family's record.
- Your passphrase and your device's keys never leave the device (except, sealed, in the recovery kit you choose to
  make).
- The tone check, the tone bar, Advice and Help & FAQ run on your device. No text is sent to any AI service.

## What the server knows

The server stores the encrypted content and the metadata it needs to deliver it: which accounts and devices exist,
who is in which family (by random IDs, never names), when things were posted and how big they are, and its own
logs (including network addresses) for security. It never sees content, names or call media.

## What DarkCipher Systems knows

Nothing. Each server is run independently by whoever installs it. The apps and the server never contact DarkCipher
Systems: no analytics, no telemetry, no adverts, no crash reports.

## Your copies

You can make court-ready reports and save copies of files to your device. Those copies are no longer encrypted: keep
them safe. Saving a copy of a file is recorded in the family's audit log, like opening it.

## Who to ask

The person who runs your server (its administrator) is responsible for it. For the software itself, see
https://darkciphersystems.com.
